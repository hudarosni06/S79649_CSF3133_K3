About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began in 1979 as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in Mengabang Telipot, providing training and research facilities for students in fisheries and marine science programs.

In July 2001, the institution was granted autonomy and officially named Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM). On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was elevated to a full-fledged public university and rebranded as Universiti Malaysia Terengganu, focusing on marine science, maritime studies, and sustainable resource management.

Today, UMT offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs supported by specialized marine laboratories and research institutes, attracting both local and international students to its coastal campus.
